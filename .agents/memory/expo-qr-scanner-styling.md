---
name: Expo QR scanner styling
description: Preserve QR scanner visual styling while keeping Expo tunnel URLs and QR files fresh.
---

The canonical QR scanner is generated by `scripts/expo-tunnel.sh`. Its visual template should load `/customer-qr.png` and `/partner-qr.png`, while the tunnel script updates those files and the visible `exp://` URL text on each successful tunnel start.

**Why:** Direct edits to `tmp-qr/scanner.html` are overwritten when Metro/ngrok restarts. Hardcoded URLs or embedded data URLs can also leave users scanning an old Partner bundle.

**How to apply:** Change the generator template first, keep the QR image paths file-based, restart the relevant Expo workflow, and verify the manifest, PNG, scanner HTML, and rendered QR page.
