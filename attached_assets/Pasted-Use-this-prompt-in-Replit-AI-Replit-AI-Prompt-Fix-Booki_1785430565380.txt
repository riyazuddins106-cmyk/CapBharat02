# Replit AI Prompt – Fix Booking Completion, Payment Status & Post-Service Workflow

## Objective

Analyze the complete booking lifecycle across:

* Customer App
* Partner App
* Admin Panel
* Backend APIs
* Database
* Notifications

Do **NOT** redesign the UI or create duplicate booking flows.

The objective is to make the booking completion and payment process reliable and synchronized across all applications.

---

# Current Problem

When the partner marks a booking as **Completed**:

* Customer receives a notification to make payment.
* The booking immediately disappears from the customer's Upcoming Bookings.
* Customer has not yet paid.
* Partner has no idea whether payment has been received.
* Admin cannot clearly identify Paid vs Unpaid completed jobs.

This creates confusion because the service is completed but payment is still pending.

## Expected Result

The booking workflow should clearly distinguish **service completion** from **payment completion**. Every stakeholder should always know the exact state of the job:

* **Customer:** Can see completed services awaiting payment and pay later.
* **Partner:** Can instantly see whether payment is **Pending** or **Received**, eliminating uncertainty.
* **Admin:** Can monitor completed jobs, outstanding collections, successful payments, and financial reports with complete visibility.
* **System:** Maintains accurate, synchronized service and payment states across the Customer App, Partner App, Admin Panel, APIs, database, notifications, reports, and analytics.
