==========================================
SPECIFIC THINGS TO LOOK FOR
==========================================

Look for these known potential issues:

A) availability_status filter — broadcast() may only select partners with 
   availabilityStatus = 'available'. If the partner's status is 'offline', 
   dispatch silently finds no candidates and returns [].

B) partnerServices mismatch — dispatch joins partnerServices to find partners 
   who offer the specific service being booked. If the partner has no rows in 
   partner_services matching the booked service IDs, they won't be found.

C) Document gate — dispatch may filter out partners who don't have approved 
   mandatory documents. Check document_type_configs table for is_mandatory=true rows.

D) listJobs query — the partner repository query must include bookings where:
   - bookingPartnerRequests.partner_id = this_partner AND status = 'pending'
   OR
   - bookings.professional_id = this_partner
   If this JOIN or WHERE condition is wrong, dispatched jobs won't show.

E) Empty bookingPartnerRequests — if dispatch returns [] (no candidates found),
   no rows are inserted, and the partner can NEVER see the booking no matter 
   what the listJobs query does.

F) No polling — if the Partner App has no refetchInterval, the partner won't 
   see new jobs unless they manually pull-to-refresh.
